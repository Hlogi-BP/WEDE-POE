Our Services

At Pfuna Vahn, we are dedicated to providing a safe haven and professional care for the vulnerable members of the Pretoria community [3, 4].

What We Provide

  • Temporary Shelter: A safe and warm environment for homeless women and children seeking refuge [3].
  • Nutrition & Clothing: We provide essential meals and clothing to those in our care, ensuring basic human dignity is restored [5].
  • Counseling & Support: Professional social work services to help individuals heal and rebuild their lives [4].

Additional Support

Beyond our primary shelter services, we focus on community outreach and social development initiatives to address the root causes of homelessness and vulnerability [3, 6].

We work closely with partners like the Salvation Army and the City of Tshwane to coordinate broader community aid [6].
