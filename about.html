Our Story

Pfuna Vanhu Help Mense Women and Child Centre is a non-profit organization based in Pretoria, South Africa. Since our founding in 2017, we have served as a homeless shelter and safe haven for vulnerable individuals in our community [2].

The center was founded by Mrs JS Mokhare, a retired social worker whose dedication to professional care and compassion laid the foundation for everything we do today [4].

Our Mission and Vision

Mission: To empower individuals and families in need by providing them with the resources and support they require to rebuild their lives and achieve self-sufficiency [3].

Vision: To create a society where every vulnerable woman and child in Pretoria has access to a safe haven and the opportunity to heal and thrive [5].
