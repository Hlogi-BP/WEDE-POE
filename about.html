Our Story

Pfuna Vanhu Help Mense Women and Child Centre is a non-profit organization based in Pretoria, South Africa, that serves as a homeless shelter and safe haven for vulnerable individuals.

Leadership

Founder: Johanna Mokhare

Project Manager and community leader dedicated to social upliftment.

History and Foundation

The center was founded by Mrs JS Mokhare, a retired social worker described as selfless and dedicated to helping her community. Her background in social work laid the foundation for the organization's mission to provide professional care and compassion to those in need.

Our Mission and Vision

Mission: Our mission is to empower individuals and families in need by providing them with the resources and support they require to rebuild their lives and achieve self-sufficiency.

Vision: We envision a community where every individual has access to basic necessities, opportunities for growth, and a supportive environment to thrive.
