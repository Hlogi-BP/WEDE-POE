# **Project Overview**



In this project i am required to help a local business, or NPO of my choosing improve or even initiate their online presence through building them a website. One that will inform and establish their online branding.



# **Website Goals and Objectives**



The main goal of the website is to establish an online presence for the organisation and increase awareness about the services they provide.



### **Website Goals**



•	Increase awareness about the organisation

•	Encourage the community support and donations

•	Provide information about services offered 

•	Promote volunteer opportunities 

•	Share updates and community initiatives 



### **Key Performative Indicators (KPIs)**



•	Achieve at least 500 website visitors per month within the first 3 months of launch

•	Generate 10–20 donation enquiries per month through the website

•	Receive 5–10 volunteer sign-ups per month via the online form

•	Increase social media engagement by 25% within 3 months through website traffic

•	Maintain an average session duration of 2+ minutes (indicating user engagement)

Current Website Analysis 

Currently the organisation has a single paged website that seems to be AI generated and hosted, with no official, professional website.



##### **Strengths:**

•	Strong community impact and proven track record

•	Dedicated leadership and governance structure 

•	Established partnerships with organisations such as the City of Tshwane and Salvation army

•	Clear mission focus – social development and community care 



##### **Weaknesses**

•	Lack of online visibility and presence 

•	Potential donors may struggle to find reliable information about the organisation

•	No central platform to display services, programmes, and impact statistics 







##### **Opportunities for improvement** 

Developing a website would help the organisation to:

•	Share detailed information about its services and programmes

•	Provide clear donation options and details such as banking information 

•	Showcase community impact through statistics and success stories 

•	Improve communication with supporters and volunteers 



#### **Proposed Website Features and Functionality**

The proposed website will include the following pages and features that will help the organisation communicate effectively with the public:



###### **Essential Pages:**

•	Homepage 

•	About Us

•	Services and Programmes 

•	Gallery / community impact

•	Volunteer Opportunities 

•	Donations and Sponsors Page

•	Contact Page 



###### **Functional Features** 

•	Donation Information and Sponsorship request(s) 

\-	The website will provide clear banking details (EFT) and downloadable sponsorship request documents. A “Donate” button will guide users to a dedicated page explaining how to contribute financially or materially.

•	Contact form 

\-	A basic HTML form allowing users to send enquiries directly to the organisation’s email address.

•	Impact statistics and success stories 

\-	Key statistics (e.g., residents supported, outreach numbers) will be displayed in structured sections to build trust and credibility.

•	Social Media Link(s)

•	Photo gallery showing outreach activities 

\-	Images of outreach activities and the shelter will be displayed to visually communicate the organisation’s impact.

•	Volunteer sign-up form 

\-	Users will complete a simple form including name, contact details, and skills. Submissions will be sent via email to the organisation for follow-up.

These features will help improve the organisation’s ability to connect with donors, sponsors, volunteers and community members.

Design and User Experience 

The website design will focus on simplicity, accessibility, and warmth to reflect the organisation’s mission.



###### **Colour scheme:**

•	Soft blue (baby/sky blue signifying trust and care)

•	Off White (clean and professional)

•	 Warm Orange (hope and community)



###### **Typography** 

•	Headings: Poppins 

•	Body text: Open Sans



###### **Layout**

The website will use a simple and accessible layout with a clear navigation menu at the top of each page. Key sections such as Donations, services, and contact information will be easy to locate.

User Experience Considerations 

•	Mobile friendly design 

•	Clear navigation for visitors 

•	Prominent donation and volunteer buttons 

•	Easy to read content structure
