Who We Are

Pfuna Vahn - Help Mense Women & Children Centre is a registered Non-Prpfit Organisation based in Pretoria, Gauteng. The Organisation was founded in 2017 by Johanna Mokhare, a retired social worker who dedicated more than 30 years of her life to public service.

We at Pfuna Vahn provide a safe haven for homeless and abused women, children and families. for the first six years, the organisation operated entirely through personal funds and community support.

Impact Since 2017

  • 468 Residents Accommodated
  • 351 Drop-in clients served
  • 9 years of community service
  • 104 successful exits
  • 178 skills developed in participants
  • 1 254 outreach contacts

Our Services

  • 24/7 resident shelter for women and children
  • 3 nutritious meals a day
  • Bathing facilities and hygiene supplies
  • Psychological counselling
  • Family reunification
  • Skills development trainig
  • Job assistance and CV drafting
  • Community outreach programmes
